# 0.2.2 — 2026-07-31

## Added

- **`Embed.embeds`** and **`Embed.omittedEmbeds`** on the `record` variant carry a quoted post's own attachments (e.g. the images on a quoted image post), up to `MAX_EMBED_DEPTH` (3); anything past that bound is counted in `omittedEmbeds` rather than dropped silently. `renderEmbedLines()` renders both, labelled to distinguish the quoted post's own attachments from `media` attached alongside the quote. ([#16](https://github.com/cyanheads/bluesky-mcp-server/issues/16))
- **`bsky_get_author_feed`** enrichment reports `originalPosts` and `reposts` whenever a page carries at least one repost, since no upstream filter excludes reposts and `limit` counts both. ([#17](https://github.com/cyanheads/bluesky-mcp-server/issues/17))
- **`pronouns`** and **`website`** on `bsky_get_profile` and the `bsky://profile/{actor}` resource, which read `profileViewDetailed`. **`pronouns`** alone on `bsky_search_actors` and `bsky_get_follows` (including its `subject`), which read `profileView` and never carry `website`. ([#22](https://github.com/cyanheads/bluesky-mcp-server/issues/22), [#23](https://github.com/cyanheads/bluesky-mcp-server/issues/23))

## Fixed

- **`bsky_get_post_thread`** now discloses when the parent chain was cut at `parent_height` instead of reaching the conversation root — previously the topmost returned parent was indistinguishable from a root. The topmost node carries `parentChainTruncated: true`, mirrored in an enrichment field and a notice naming the AT-URI to re-root at. ([#20](https://github.com/cyanheads/bluesky-mcp-server/issues/20))
- **`bsky_get_post_thread`**'s rendered reply depth now rides the author heading (`### ↳2 Name (@handle)`) instead of a two-space-per-level left-margin indent, which pushed every line of a depth-2+ node past CommonMark's four-space code-block threshold and turned headings, quotes, and truncation notices into literal text. ([#21](https://github.com/cyanheads/bluesky-mcp-server/issues/21))
